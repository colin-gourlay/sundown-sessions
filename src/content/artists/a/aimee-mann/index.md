## About

Aimee Mann is an American singer-songwriter whose precise, sardonic writing often follows lonely characters through disappointment, self-deception and hard-won clarity. After fronting 'Til Tuesday and writing their breakthrough single "Voices Carry", she began a solo catalogue that moves between guitar-led alternative rock, chamber pop and understated folk.

Albums including *Whatever*, *Bachelor No. 2* and *Mental Illness*, together with her songs for Paul Thomas Anderson's *Magnolia*, established Mann as an exacting independent artist with a gift for pairing difficult emotional territory with elegant melodies.
