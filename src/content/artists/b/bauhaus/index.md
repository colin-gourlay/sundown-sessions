## About

Bauhaus formed in Northampton in 1978 with Peter Murphy, Daniel Ash, David J and Kevin Haskins. Their spacious, abrasive post-punk drew on dub, glam, funk and art-school theatre; the 1979 single “Bela Lugosi's Dead” became an enduring gothic touchstone. Albums including *In the Flat Field* and *Mask* pushed that language in different directions, while their charged cover of David Bowie's “Ziggy Stardust” brought it to a wider audience. The group first split in 1983 and later reunited for several distinct periods.
