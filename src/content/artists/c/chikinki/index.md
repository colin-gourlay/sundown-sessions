## About

Chikinki are an English electronic rock band formed in Bristol. Their music brings synthesisers, wiry guitars and dance-floor momentum together, with *Lick Your Ticket* and *Brace, Brace* capturing a sound that sits between indie rock and electro-pop. “You Said” shows their instinct for tension, movement and an immediate hook.
