## About

Cocteau Twins formed in Grangemouth in 1979, with guitarist Robin Guthrie and bassist Will Heggie joined by vocalist Elizabeth Fraser; Simon Raymonde became the third member after Heggie's departure. Across records for 4AD, the trio transformed layered guitar effects, drum machines and Fraser's elastic voice into a language that helped define dream pop. The 1985 EP *Tiny Dynamine* and albums such as *Treasure* and *Heaven or Las Vegas* show that sound continually opening out. The band ended in 1997.
