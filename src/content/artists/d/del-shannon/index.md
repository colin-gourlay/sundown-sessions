## About

Del Shannon was an American singer, songwriter and guitarist from Michigan whose country influences fed into an urgent rock 'n' roll style. His 1961 breakthrough "Runaway" paired a soaring falsetto with Max Crook's Musitron keyboard, while "Hats Off to Larry", "Little Town Flirt" and "Keep Searchin'" carried his tense, lovelorn pop into the decade. His version of "Handy Man" brought that distinctive voice to another early rock 'n' roll favourite.

Beyond the singles, Shannon recorded a tribute to Hank Williams and explored psychedelic pop on *The Further Adventures of Charles Westover*. He also worked as a producer for Brian Hyland before returning with the Tom Petty-produced *Drop Down and Get Me*, which included "Sea of Love". Later sessions with Petty and Jeff Lynne became the posthumous *Rock On!*, extending a catalogue that connects country roots, early pop experimentation and guitar rock.
