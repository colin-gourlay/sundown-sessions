## About

Doves are Jimi Goodwin and twin brothers Jez and Andy Williams, a Manchester trio who emerged from the electronic group Sub Sub. Their records join rain-streaked guitar music to a producer's sense of space and rhythm, from *Lost Souls* and *The Last Broadcast* through *Some Cities*. After two extended pauses, they returned with *The Universal Want* in 2020 and *Constellations for the Lonely* in 2025.
