## About

Elvis Costello & The Attractions brought together Costello's tightly wound songwriting with Steve Nieve's inventive keyboards, Bruce Thomas's melodic bass and Pete Thomas's forceful, flexible drumming. Their performances could pivot from clipped new wave to soul, country and expansive pop without losing their urgency.

The run from *This Year's Model* through *Armed Forces*, *Get Happy!!* and *Imperial Bedroom* shows a band continually stretching its language. "(I Don't Want to Go to) Chelsea" captures their early sound at its most tense, economical and exhilarating.
