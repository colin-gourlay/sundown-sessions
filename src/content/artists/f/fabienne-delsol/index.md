## About

Fabienne Delsol is a French singer based in England whose work draws on garage rock, psychedelia and the melodic detail of 1960s pop. After fronting the Bristols, she developed a solo catalogue shaped by analogue production and an instinct for concise, memorable songs.

Records including *No Time for Sorrows*, *Between You and Me* and *On My Mind* pair period atmosphere with a voice and perspective that remain unmistakably hers. "Ragunboneman" captures that balance of garage grit, melodic charm and cool restraint.
