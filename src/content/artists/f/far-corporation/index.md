## About

Far Corporation were an international studio project assembled by German producer Frank Farian. Built around a changing cast that included musicians connected to Toto and other established groups, the project favoured grand arrangements and high-gloss rock production. Their 1985 recording of Led Zeppelin's “Stairway to Heaven” remains their best-known release, reaching the UK top ten.
