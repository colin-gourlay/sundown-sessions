## About

Franz Ferdinand are a Scottish band formed in Glasgow in 2002 around Alex Kapranos, Bob Hardy, Nick McCarthy and Paul Thomson, pulling art-school attitude, post-punk tension and disco rhythm into concise guitar pop. Their 2004 debut made that aesthetic immediate: "Take Me Out" pivots from wiry stop-start guitar into a strutting dance-rock groove, while "The Dark of the Matinée" and "Michael" underline how theatrical hooks and clipped rhythms became their signature.

Later line-ups, with Kapranos and Hardy joined by Julian Corrie, Dino Bardot and Audrey Tait, have kept that language in motion across records from the taut rush of *You Could Have It So Much Better* and the electronic lean of *Tonight: Franz Ferdinand* to *Always Ascending* and *The Human Fear*. On Sundown Sessions, "Take Me Out" keeps the link to their self-titled debut and to the angular, danceable side of early-2000s UK indie.
