## About

Gaye Bykers On Acid formed in Leicester in the mid-1980s and became closely associated with the grebo scene, mixing punk and psychedelic rock with samples, dance rhythms and a deliberately unruly visual identity. Early releases including *Everythang's Groovy* and the *Nosedive* EP established their taste for collision rather than stylistic purity.

Across *Drill Your Own Hole*, *Stewed to the Gills*, *Cancer Planet Mission* and *Pernicious Nonsense*, the band moved between noisy alternative rock, electronics and harder-edged material while retaining a mischievous punk sensibility. Their use of alter egos such as Rektüm and Lesbian Dopeheads on Mopeds extended the same irreverence beyond the records themselves.
