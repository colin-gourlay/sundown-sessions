## About

Johnny Cash was an American singer-songwriter whose deep baritone and spare, train-like rhythms became central to country music while reaching far beyond it. Songs such as "I Walk the Line", "Folsom Prison Blues" and "Man in Black" joined rebellion, faith, humour and an enduring concern for people at society's margins.

His prison concerts and work with the Tennessee Three established his commanding live presence. Decades later, the *American Recordings* series placed that voice against songs from many traditions; his interpretation of Nine Inch Nails' "Hurt" became one of his most affecting final statements.
