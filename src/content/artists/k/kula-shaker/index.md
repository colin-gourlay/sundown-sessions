## About

Kula Shaker are an English psychedelic rock band led by Crispian Mills, with Alonza Bevan, Paul Winter-Hart and Jay Darlington in the reunited classic line-up. Their 1990s breakthrough joined loud guitars and pop immediacy to sitar, tabla and Sanskrit lyrics on songs including “Tattva”, “Govinda” and their cover of “Hush”. After an initial 1999 split, the band returned to recording and touring; *Natural Magick* arrived in 2024 and *Wormslayer* in 2026.
