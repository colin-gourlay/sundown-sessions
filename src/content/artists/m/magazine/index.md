## About

Magazine formed after Howard Devoto left Buzzcocks and pursued a more expansive musical language with guitarist John McGeoch, bassist Barry Adamson, keyboard player Dave Formula and drummer John Doyle. Their songs replace punk's direct attack with tension, space and unsettling sophistication.

The debut album *Real Life* helped define post-punk, while *Secondhand Daylight* and *The Correct Use of Soap* pushed further into fractured funk, art rock and electronic atmosphere. "The Light Pours Out of Me" remains a potent example of the band's controlled intensity.
