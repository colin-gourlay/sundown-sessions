## About

Muse formed in Teignmouth, Devon, in 1994 and remain the trio of Matt Bellamy, Chris Wolstenholme and Dominic Howard. Their music expanded from the tense alternative rock of *Showbiz* into the classical sweep of *Origin of Symmetry* and *Absolution*, then absorbed electronic pop, progressive structures and stadium-scale production. “Sing for Absolution” captures the dramatic control at the heart of that evolution.
