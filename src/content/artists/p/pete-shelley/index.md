## About

Pete Shelley was an English singer, songwriter and guitarist who co-founded Buzzcocks and gave punk some of its most concise, vulnerable pop songs. His writing joined romantic uncertainty and self-questioning to sharp hooks on records including "Ever Fallen in Love (With Someone You Shouldn't've)" and "What Do I Get?".

Shelley's solo work made his long-standing interest in electronic music more visible. "Homosapien" places an intimate, questioning lyric inside a precise synth-pop arrangement, preserving the melodic directness of Buzzcocks while opening a distinctly different sonic path.
