## About

Spoon formed in Austin around Britt Daniel and Jim Eno, developing a rock sound defined less by excess than by placement: clipped rhythms, dry guitar figures, sudden spaces and melodies that arrive at unexpected angles. That economy became the band's signature as their catalogue moved through indie rock, post-punk and more rhythmically adventurous pop.

Albums including *Girls Can Tell*, *Kill the Moonlight*, *Gimme Fiction* and *Ga Ga Ga Ga Ga* established a remarkably consistent run, while later records continued to reshape the same lean vocabulary rather than simply repeat it.
