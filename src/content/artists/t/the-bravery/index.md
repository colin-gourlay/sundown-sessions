## About

The Bravery formed in New York City in 2003, with Sam Endicott's vocals set against Michael Zakarin's guitar, John Conway's keyboards, Mike Hindert's bass and Anthony Burulcich's drums. Their self-titled 2005 debut turned post-punk and new-wave influences into concise dance-rock, led by “An Honest Mistake”. Two further albums followed before a long period of inactivity; the group announced its return in 2021.
