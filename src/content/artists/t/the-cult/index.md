## About

The Cult grew from the post-punk world around Ian Astbury and Billy Duffy, evolving from the darker atmosphere of Death Cult into a band capable of combining gothic texture with increasingly direct rock power. Duffy's ringing guitar and Astbury's commanding voice became the constants as the group's sound expanded.

*Dreamtime* and *Love* established their early identity through songs such as "Spiritwalker" and "She Sells Sanctuary"; *Electric* and *Sonic Temple* pushed toward harder, widescreen rock without losing that sense of drama. Their catalogue has continued through reunions and later records including *Under the Midnight Sun*.
