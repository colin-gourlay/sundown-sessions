## About

The Receiving End were a Scottish guitar band fronted by Danny Holland, with Bill Thaw and Ian Douglas in the line-up documented by Sundown Sessions. The surviving recordings heard in Show #2 include “That's the Way I Operate!”, “He's the Man” and “Nothing's Wrong”. Public documentation is limited, so this profile deliberately records only details supported by the broadcast and its source material.
