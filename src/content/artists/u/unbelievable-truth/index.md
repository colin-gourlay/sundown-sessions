## About

Unbelievable Truth formed in Oxford around Andy Yorke, Nigel Powell and Jason Moulster, developing a restrained alternative-rock sound built from acoustic space, melancholy melody and quietly intense performances. Rather than chasing the louder dynamics associated with much late-1990s British guitar music, their songs often let small shifts in texture and voice carry the emotional weight.

Their debut album *Almost Here* established that character, with "Building" showing how the band could turn understatement into tension. The group later returned to recording and live work after its original run, preserving a catalogue whose intimacy remains its defining strength.
