## About

Westlife formed in Ireland in 1998 and built a long-running pop career around the voices of Shane Filan, Mark Feehily, Kian Egan and Nicky Byrne, with Brian McFadden part of the original five-piece line-up. Their sound centres on close harmonies, large-scale ballads and direct melodic songwriting.

From their self-titled debut through *Coast to Coast* and *World of Our Own*, the group became a fixture of UK and Irish pop before splitting in 2012. They reunited in 2018 and returned to recording and touring, extending a catalogue that includes "Flying Without Wings", "World of Our Own" and "Unbreakable".
