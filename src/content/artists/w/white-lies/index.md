## About

White Lies are a West London trio formed by Harry McVeigh, Charles Cave and Jack Lawrence-Brown after their earlier band Fear of Flying. Their 2009 debut *To Lose My Life...* set McVeigh's deep voice against urgent bass, bright synthesisers and widescreen choruses. Subsequent records have kept that post-punk tension while widening the band's electronic and melodic range.
