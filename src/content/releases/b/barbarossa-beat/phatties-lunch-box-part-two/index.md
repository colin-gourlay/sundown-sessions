## About

*Phattie's Lunch Box Part Two* opens the Barbarossa Beat project, bringing drummer Dave Barbarossa together with Mekon's John Gosling. Built around live drums and breakbeat instincts, the track reconnects two musicians whose histories stretch across punk, post-punk, club culture and electronic experimentation.

The single was conceived as the first instalment in a run of collaborations, placing Barbarossa's playing at the centre rather than treating the drums as background texture.
