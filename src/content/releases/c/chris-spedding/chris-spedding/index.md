## About

Chris Spedding's self-titled 1976 album puts the celebrated session guitarist firmly at the centre of the frame. The record pares his playing back to concise rock-and-roll songs, sharp riffs and a dry sense of swagger rather than turning into a display of instrumental excess.

“Motor Bikin'” had already established Spedding as a performer in his own right, and its appearance here makes it a natural route into an album that captures the guitarist's direct, economical approach at its most accessible.
