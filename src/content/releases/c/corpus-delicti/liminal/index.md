## About

More than three decades after Corpus Delicti first emerged from the French gothic underground, *Liminal* finds the band returning with a record that feels purposeful rather than nostalgic. The album keeps the tension and atmosphere of their earlier work while allowing the arrangements more space and weight.

The seven-minute title track closes the record in expansive fashion, making it a particularly effective Sundown Sessions selection: dark, patient and willing to let atmosphere develop rather than rush toward a hook.
