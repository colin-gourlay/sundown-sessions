## About

Released as a digital single on 29 July 2022, *Geordie Munro* is billed to David Latto & The Lang Spoons. The recording sets its Fife-rooted story in a compact indie-rock arrangement, with the recurring choice between Idaho and Kirkcaldy giving the song its local character and memorable refrain.

The 3:05 single was heard in each of the first three Sundown Sessions shows. Its references to Fife and Kirkcaldy make it a particularly natural fit for the programme, while the full artist credit preserves The Lang Spoons' part in the release.
