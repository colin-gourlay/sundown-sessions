## About

*Indigo Red* is Fabienne DelSol's fifth solo album, continuing the blend of garage pop, psychedelia, French yé-yé and beat-era influences that has run through her work for more than two decades. Recorded at Gizzard Studios with Ed Deegan, it mixes originals with carefully chosen interpretations rather than treating vintage sounds as museum pieces.

“She Is Gone” arrived ahead of the album on 18 June 2026, so its appearance on the 7 July Sundown Sessions broadcast was an advance glimpse of a record that followed three days later.
