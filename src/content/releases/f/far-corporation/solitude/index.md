## About

*Solitude* is Far Corporation's 1994 album, revisiting material associated with the multinational studio project assembled by producer Frank Farian. The Show #2 selection is the extended “Stairway to Heaven”, a recording first issued in 1985 and notable as the first cover of the Led Zeppelin song to reach the UK singles chart. This page represents the 1994 album source used by the playlist, not the earlier single as a separate release.
