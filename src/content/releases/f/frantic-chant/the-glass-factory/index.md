## About

*The Glass Factory* is a sprawling 21-track double album from Edinburgh's Frantic Chant, allowing the band's psychedelic instincts to stretch far beyond conventional song lengths. Guitars, effects and unexpected instrumentation push the record between indie rock, shoegaze and freewheeling psych without settling comfortably in one place.

“Mushroom Jim & The Planet Of The Funky Apes” is exactly the kind of deep, long-form selection the Track Guide is designed to preserve: an eight-minute detour that gives the broadcast room to wander.
