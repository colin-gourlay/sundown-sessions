## About

*DAGGER* carries IST IST's stark Manchester post-punk into a tighter, more immediate setting. The record balances clipped rhythms and brooding atmosphere with the band's instinct for melodies that emerge gradually from the tension.

“Makes No Difference” arrives near the beginning of the album and works as a concise entry point into this phase of the band: controlled, dark-edged and recognisably IST IST without simply repeating what came before.
