## About

*Cabinet of Curiosities* is the album that introduced Jacco Gardner's richly detailed pop world to Sundown Sessions. “Clear The Air”, “The One Eyed King” and “The Ballad of Little Jane” all show how confidently he balances melody, atmosphere and vintage studio colour across the record.

As a debut, it stands out for the way its baroque textures and psychedelic haze still feel intimate rather than ornamental. The songs are carefully arranged, but they never lose their warmth, making this the clearest starting point for listeners tracing the shape of Gardner's catalogue.
