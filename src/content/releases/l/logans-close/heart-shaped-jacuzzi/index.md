## About

*Heart-Shaped Jacuzzi* is Logan's Close's debut album, a record that turns the Edinburgh band's sharp melodic instincts and off-centre humour into a full-length statement. The songs move between wiry guitars, characterful arrangements and a distinctly local sense of personality.

“Hot Blondes In Your Area Tonight” opens the album and immediately establishes that tone, making it a natural Sundown Sessions selection and a useful doorway into the rest of the record.
