## About

*Audition* is the debut album from Redemption ZERO, a collaborative post-punk project bringing together musicians with roots across the goth and alternative scenes. Guest voices and players give the record a shifting character while a dark, dramatic core holds the nine tracks together.

“The Bitter Taste Of Torment” features Andrea Stewart and is one of the album's original compositions, combining a strong vocal presence with the project's brooding guitar-led atmosphere.
