## About the Album

*Propaganda* catches Sparks at full creative velocity. Arriving only months
after *Kimono My House*, it turns Ron Mael’s tightly wound songs and Russell
Mael’s theatrical vocals into a brisk sequence of glam-rock drama, barbed pop
hooks and sudden shifts in mood.

The miniature title track snaps directly into “At Home, At Work, At Play”,
setting the album’s restless pace. Across the record, producer Muff Winwood
keeps the arrangements sharp enough for the Mael brothers’ wit to land without
losing the music’s exuberance.
