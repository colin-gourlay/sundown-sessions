## About

*Argybargy* catches Squeeze at the point where Chris Difford and Glenn Tilbrook's songwriting was becoming both sharper and more assured. Character sketches, romantic frustration and finely compressed melodies sit inside arrangements that make cleverness feel effortless.

“Another Nail In My Heart” is one of the album's clearest examples: intricate enough to reward close listening, but concise and immediate enough to land as pure pop.
