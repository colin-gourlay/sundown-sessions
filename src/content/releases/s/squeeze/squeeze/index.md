## About

Squeeze's self-titled debut catches the Deptford band before their pop instincts had fully settled into the shape that would define them. John Cale produced most of the album, drawing out its wiry, restless edge, while the band took the controls for “Bang Bang” and “Take Me I’m Yours”. That split is revealing: the latter's clipped rhythm, close harmonies and rushing momentum point straight towards the sharply observed singles that followed.

The version of “Take Me I’m Yours” heard in Show #1 is the 3:46 album recording from the original UK A&M LP, not the shorter 2:45 single edit. Within the album's rougher experiments, it is the moment where Chris Difford and Glenn Tilbrook's partnership snaps decisively into focus.
