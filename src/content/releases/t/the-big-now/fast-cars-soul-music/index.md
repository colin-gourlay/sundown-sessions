## About

*Fast Cars, Soul Music* is a demo cassette by The Big Now, a Scottish band active around 1989. The recording preserves two guitar-led alternative rock songs that appeared in the first Sundown Sessions broadcast, where the band were introduced as the Artist Spotlight.

## Tracks Featured on Sundown Sessions

- Fast Cars, Soul Music
