## About

*A Date With Elvis* brought The Cramps back to the studio after a long gap and gave their mutant rockabilly a bigger, clearer sound without taming its sleaze or humour. Poison Ivy's guitar remains the engine while Lux Interior pushes every lyric toward camp, menace or glorious bad taste.

“Blue Moon Baby” appears on later expanded editions rather than the core eleven-track UK LP, but it belongs naturally to the same Cramps universe: vintage rock and roll pulled apart and rebuilt in their own image.
