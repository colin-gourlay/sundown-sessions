## About

*Airport - The Motors Greatest Hits* puts the obvious hit up front, but its real strength is the shape it gives The Motors’ short, restless catalogue. The six-minute charge of “Dancing The Night Away” leads into the bright power pop of “Airport” and the more polished ambition of *Tenement Steps*, with a live “Cold Love” and other rarities widening the view beyond the singles.

For Sundown Sessions, “Dancing The Night Away” is the doorway into that story. Across one disc, the compilation catches a band moving quickly from pub-rock muscle towards new-wave economy without losing its instinct for oversized melodies, making it a concise introduction rather than simply a roll-call of hits.
