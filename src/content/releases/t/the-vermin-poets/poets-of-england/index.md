## About

*Poets Of England* is where The Vermin Poets hit their stride — a short, punchy record that packs twelve songs into under forty minutes without a wasted moment. Released in 2010 on Damaged Goods, the album channels the roughed-up energy of early garage punk through an English underground tradition, with Billy Childish's guest bass on "Like Poets Often Do" underscoring the band's lineage and lending the track a particular gravity. That song is a natural entry point: urgent, direct, and representative of everything the album does well.

Beyond that highlight, *Poets Of England* rewards the full listen. The arrangements are lean, the performances ragged in exactly the right way, and there is a wit running through the lyrics — from the blunt attack of "Baby Booming Bastards" to the slow-burning title track — that sets the band apart from straightforward revivalists. It is a record that earns its Damaged Goods imprint with conviction.
