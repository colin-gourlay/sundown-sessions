## About

*The W Factor* gathers together the best-known songs associated with The Wombles, the musical project built around Mike Batt's songs for the much-loved characters. The collection moves from the instantly recognisable “The Wombling Song” through a catalogue that mixed playful pop craft with surprisingly ambitious arrangements.

For Sundown Sessions, “The Wombling Song” makes an intentionally unexpected opening statement: familiar, colourful and immediately capable of sending the broadcast somewhere less predictable.
