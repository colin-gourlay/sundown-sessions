## About

*New Romantic, Set 1* is a 2022 AudioSparx compilation bringing together twenty tracks under a broad new-wave and New Romantic banner. Its value to this broadcast is more specific: the collection provides the version of Twisted Nerve's “Seance” used in the playlist.

The track itself reaches much further back than the compilation. Twisted Nerve originally issued *Séance* in 1984, so the appearance here connects a modern digital collection with a cult strand of Scottish post-punk history.
