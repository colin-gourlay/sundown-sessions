## About

Whispering Sons' debut album *Image* turns alienation and unease into something physical. Fenne Kuppens' commanding vocal presence sits against taut basslines, stark guitars and arrangements that repeatedly build pressure without losing their sense of space.

“Dense” arrives late in the record and distils that atmosphere into a compact, claustrophobic piece. Within Sundown Sessions it deepens the darker post-punk thread while opening a route into one of Belgium's most distinctive contemporary bands.
