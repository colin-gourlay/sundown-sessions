## About

“Champagne on Christmas Day (live)” is the 2:29 performance from Becky Becky's [Art School Dancing](/releases/b/becky-becky/art-school-dancing/) EP, released on 30 March 2015. Recorded for Ally Gourlay's final Art School Dancing radio show, it featured in [Show #4, broadcast on 26 June 2024](/shows/featuring-kenny-armour-from-andysmanclub/).
