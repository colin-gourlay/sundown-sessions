## About

“House of the Black Madonna (live)” is the 10:16 performance from Becky Becky's [Art School Dancing](/releases/b/becky-becky/art-school-dancing/) EP, released on 30 March 2015. Recorded for Ally Gourlay's final Art School Dancing radio show, it featured in [Show #1, broadcast on 5 June 2024](/shows/featuring-the-big-now/). This extended performance is distinct from the studio recording on *Good Morning, Midnight*.
