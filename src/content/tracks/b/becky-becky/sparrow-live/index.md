## About

“Sparrow (live)” is the 4:07 performance from Becky Becky's [Art School Dancing](/releases/b/becky-becky/art-school-dancing/) EP, released on 30 March 2015. Recorded for Ally Gourlay's final Art School Dancing radio show, it featured in [Show #6, broadcast on 24 July 2024](/shows/featuring-the-thieves/).
