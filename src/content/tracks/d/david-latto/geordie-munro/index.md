## About

“Geordie Munro” is a 3:05 indie-rock recording credited to [David Latto & The Lang Spoons](/artists/d/david-latto/). Released as a [digital single](/releases/d/david-latto/geordie-munro/) on 29 July 2022, its Fife-rooted story and recurring choice between Idaho and Kirkcaldy give the song its local character.

The track featured in each of the first three Sundown Sessions shows.
