## About

"Slow Hands" is the fifth track on Interpol's second album, [Antics](/releases/i/interpol/antics/) (2004). Its taut rhythm and interlocking guitars bring a direct, propulsive edge to the band's shadowy sound. It featured in [Show #1](/shows/1/).
