## About

“Fat Cats Drown in Milk” is the second track on IST IST’s second album, [The Art of Lying](/releases/i/ist-ist/the-art-of-lying/) (2021). Across its 3:19 runtime, the song channels the Manchester band’s brooding post-punk through a taut, driving arrangement.
