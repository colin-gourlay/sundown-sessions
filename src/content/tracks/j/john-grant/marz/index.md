## About

"Marz" is the second track on [John Grant's](/artists/j/john-grant/) debut solo album, [Queen of Denmark](/releases/j/john-grant/queen-of-denmark/) (2010). Its tender piano-led arrangement and rich baritone bring a quietly wistful pause to [Show #1](/shows/featuring-the-big-now/).
