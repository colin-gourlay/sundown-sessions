## About

“Cuddly Toy” is the third track on [Roachford's self-titled debut album](/releases/r/roachford/roachford/). The documented 1988 UK CBS LP, catalogue number 460630 1, lists the recording at 3:49; the copy played in Show #1 runs to 3:48, reflecting the broadcast file timing rather than a different album edition.
