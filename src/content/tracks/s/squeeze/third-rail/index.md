## About

“Third Rail” is the third track on [Squeeze's](/artists/s/squeeze/)
1993 album [Some Fantastic Place](/releases/s/squeeze/some-fantastic-place/).
It featured on [Show #11 — A celebration of Elektra Records](/shows/featuring-a-celebration-of-elektra-records/),
broadcast on 28 August 2024.
