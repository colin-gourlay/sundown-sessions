## About

"Dancing The Night Away" opens [Airport - The Motors Greatest Hits](/releases/t/the-motors/airport-the-motors-greatest-hits/), the 1995 compilation selected for [Show #1](/shows/featuring-the-big-now/). Its six-minute rush of driving guitars and oversized melody captures [The Motors'](/artists/t/the-motors/) mix of pub-rock grit and power-pop ambition.
